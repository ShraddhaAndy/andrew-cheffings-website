About the Author

Andrew Cheffings

Andrew Cheffings is an author of LGBTQ+ fiction. He was born on a small working farm on the Lincolnshire Marsh, a landscape that runs through nearly everything he writes.

His family history includes stories passed down through generations — among them his grandfather's North Lincolnshire dialect and the tales that came with it — and his writing reflects growing up within a changing rural culture, rich in stories and tradition.

Stories rooted in place.

Andrew's novels sit at the meeting point of local place and culture seen through a queer lens, queer romance, and a spirituality grounded in queer ecology.

In this novel, Andrew Cheffings explores how subgroups can form within society, becoming coercive and creating intense power structures that are difficult to escape. Harp Wood

In the 1980s, following decades of agricultural and cultural decline on the Lincolnshire Marsh, there was a sudden and unexpected springing of optimism. People opened odd businesses in remote houses far along marsh lanes — getting crafty, making strange objects from wood, taking moody photographs of collapsed buildings.
