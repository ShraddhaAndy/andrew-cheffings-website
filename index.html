About the Author

Andrew Cheffings

Andrew Cheffings is an author of LGBTQ+ fiction. He was born on a small working farm on the Lincolnshire Marsh, a landscape that shapes nearly everything he writes.

His family heritage includes his grandfather's North Lincolnshire dialect stories passed down aurally through the generations — and his writing reflects growing up within a declining local rural culture, rich in tradition.

Stories rooted in place.

Andrew's novels sit at the meeting point of local place and culture seen through a queer lens, queer romance, and a spirituality grounded in queer ecology.

Literary Fiction

The House with the Round Window

In this novel, Andrew Cheffings explores how subgroups can form within society, becoming coercive and creating intense power structures that are difficult to escape. John is a young middle-aged man working at a Community Music Centre on the Lincolnshire Marsh, struggling to deal with the challenging vision of his new boss and the results of the local gay intrigue which follows in its wake.

Literary Fiction · Place

Harp Wood

In the 1980s, following decades of agricultural and cultural decline on the Lincolnshire Marsh, there was a sudden and unexpected springing of optimism. People opened odd businesses in remote houses far along marsh lanes — getting crafty, making strange objects from wood and following their hearts in new, queerer directions.
